# Research

Research to support the argument, not to accumulate facts. Start with the core question and list the claims needed to answer it.

## Source ledger

| ID | Claim | Source | Date/scope | Evidence type | Direct support | Uncertainty |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

For each statistic capture unit, denominator, time period, geography, and comparison baseline. Prefer primary sources, standards, official data, direct experiments, and transparent methodology. For high-stakes or contested claims, seek an independent second evidence mode.

## Stop rule

Stop when the mechanism is supported, the strongest reasonable alternative is addressed, and new research no longer changes the outline. Mark unknowns; never fill them with plausible-sounding detail.
