# Pacing and Cognitive Load

Density follows dependency, not a fixed sentence count. Slow down at the first appearance of a concept, unit, or causal link; compress repeated logistics after the model is stable.

When two dense blocks are adjacent, insert one of: worked example, concrete consequence, diagram cue, one-sentence recap, or step boundary. Shorten repetition before deleting the bridge that makes the argument valid. Read the densest run aloud and mark where a viewer must hold too many new entities.
