# Hooks

Choose one family based on the evidence and audience:

- Scale/number: a sourced magnitude that changes the frame.
- Explicit question: a narrow, answerable dependency.
- Expectation gap: ordinary belief followed by a specific mismatch.
- Concrete scene/object: an immediate action containing the puzzle.
- Direct context: plain orientation when the mechanism is the value.

Write `assumption → mismatch → unanswered dependency → promise of model`. The first 1–3 blocks should establish the gap; do not use an unrelated shock fact, reveal the whole answer, or invent urgency.
